# NetAtlas User Guide & Platform Walkthrough

## 1. Navigating the Global Map View

The **Global Map Canvas** is the central research experience of NetAtlas.

1. **Map Navigation:** Use standard pan and zoom gestures (mouse wheel or pinch). The projection supports seamless worldwide panning.
2. **Layer Visibility Control:** In the floating panel on the top-right, toggle individual infrastructure layers:
   - **Submarine Cable Routes:** Cyan/blue solid lines for operational routes; orange dashed lines for planned systems.
   - **Landing Stations:** Blue circle points with white halos representing coastal termination facilities.
   - **Colocation Facilities:** Teal markers grouped dynamically into spatial clusters.
3. **Interactive Inspection:**
   - **Click any cable line:** The bottom-left inspector reveals the cable name, RFS year, total length in km, planned status, landing point count, and operating consortium.
   - **Click any landing point or facility:** Inspect municipal location, operator attribution, and geographic coordinates.
4. **Universal Search Bar:**
   - Enter cable names (e.g., `2Africa`, `SEA-ME-WE`), exchange points (e.g., `DE-CIX`, `LINX`), or cities (e.g., `Mumbai`, `London`, `Tokyo`).
   - Select results from the instant autocomplete dropdown to immediately inspect attributes.
5. **Economy Selector:**
   - Select any country from the dropdown (e.g., `India`, `United States`, `Germany`) to automatically fly the camera to its geographic centroid.

## 2. Navigating Secondary Research Views

- **Submarine Cables View:** Explore a tabular catalog of all 713 verified systems with sorting by length, year, and operational status.
- **Internet Exchanges View:** Search and analyze 1,337 public IXPs worldwide, including participant ASN counts, physical facilities, and IPv6 dual-stack support.
- **Colocation Facilities View:** Filter 3,000 colocation sites and data centers by operating entity, city, and connected network depth.
- **Topology & Graphs View:** Review NetworkX analytical charts comparing top IXP hubs (DE-CIX Frankfurt, LINX, AMS-IX), hyperscaler transit presences (Hurricane Electric, Cloudflare, Microsoft), and the power-law degree distribution.
- **Regional Density View:** Compare the infrastructure depth of continental macro-regions using the composite Infrastructure Density Index.
- **Data Stories View:** Read evidence-backed research narratives synthesizing global routing gravity, maritime subsea chokepoints, and commercial peering concentration.
- **Data Quality & Provenance View:** Review automated data completeness metrics and authoritative licensing terms for PeeringDB, TeleGeography, and OpenStreetMap.
